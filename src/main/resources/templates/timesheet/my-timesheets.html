How to Use Timesheets
Create Timesheet

Click "Create New Timesheet" to start a new timesheet for a pay period. Add your time entries and expenses.

Submit for Approval

Once complete, submit your timesheet for manager approval. You can edit drafts but not submitted timesheets.

Track Status

Monitor your timesheet status: Draft → Submitted → Approved → Paid. View details and print approved timesheets.
