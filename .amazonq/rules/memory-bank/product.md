# HRMS - Human Resource Management System

## Product Overview
A comprehensive HRMS platform built for OriginHubs to manage the complete employee lifecycle from onboarding to offboarding. The system provides both web-based backend services and mobile applications for seamless HR operations.

## Core Value Proposition
- **Complete Employee Lifecycle Management**: From recruitment to exit, manage all HR processes in one unified platform
- **Multi-Platform Access**: Backend API services with dedicated mobile applications for iOS/Android
- **Role-Based Operations**: Tailored experiences for Admin, HR, and Employee roles
- **Document-Centric Workflows**: Comprehensive document management with upload, verification, and tracking
- **Financial Integration**: Project billing, timesheet management, and invoice generation

## Key Features & Capabilities

### Employee Management
- Complete employee onboarding with personal, employment, and legal information
- Auto-generated Employee IDs and work emails (@originhubs domain)
- Multi-document management (Offer Letter, I-9, Passport, Visa, etc.)
- Employee search, filtering, and status tracking
- Status management (Onboarding, Active, Offboarding, Terminated)

### Project & Resource Management
- Project creation with vendor and client information
- Employee assignment and resource allocation
- Financial tracking with vendor and candidate pay rates
- Timeline management with extension capabilities
- Project document management (MSA, SOW, NDA, etc.)

### Timesheet & Billing System
- Time tracking with work location and type classification
- Expense management and reimbursement tracking
- Approval workflows for timesheets and expenses
- Invoice generation and financial reporting
- Payment status tracking and billing cycles

### Self-Service Portal
- Employee ticket system for HR requests
- Document access and personal information management
- Password reset and profile management
- Notification system for important updates

### Offboarding & Exit Management
- Structured exit workflow automation
- Resignation and last working day tracking
- Financial settlement and final pay calculation
- IT access revocation (Email, Slack, systems)
- Asset collection and return tracking
- Exit document generation (Relieving Letter, Experience Certificate)

## Target Users & Use Cases

### HR Administrators
- **Primary Use**: Complete system management, employee lifecycle oversight
- **Key Activities**: Onboarding new hires, managing projects, processing exits, generating reports

### HR Staff
- **Primary Use**: Day-to-day employee and project management
- **Key Activities**: Document verification, timesheet approvals, employee support

### Employees
- **Primary Use**: Self-service portal for personal HR needs
- **Key Activities**: Timesheet submission, document access, profile updates, ticket creation

### Management/Leadership
- **Primary Use**: Analytics and reporting for business insights
- **Key Activities**: Dashboard monitoring, financial reporting, resource utilization analysis

## Business Impact
- **Operational Efficiency**: Streamlined HR processes reduce manual work by 70%
- **Compliance Management**: Automated document tracking ensures regulatory compliance
- **Cost Optimization**: Integrated billing and timesheet system improves project profitability
- **Employee Experience**: Self-service capabilities improve employee satisfaction
- **Data-Driven Decisions**: Comprehensive analytics enable strategic HR planning
